Privacy notice

UniGuide prototype, last updated November 2026

UniGuide is a student-built prototype, not an official university service.

Without an account

You can use UniGuide without creating an account. Your answers stay in your browser and are not sent to or stored on our server.

If you create an account

If you create an account and agree to save your plan, we store:

  • your email address, and your password in hashed form (we never store or see the password itself)
  • your university
  • your answers to the questions, which may include health information such as mental health, conditions, allergies and disability
  • which checklist items you've ticked off
  • the date you gave consent

We only store your answers after you explicitly agree. We use them for one purpose only: to show you your plan again and save your progress.

Who can see it

Only you. Every request to our server checks that you're signed in, and you can only read or change your own plan. We don't sell or share your data, and it isn't used for advertising.

Where it's stored

Our server is hosted by Render (Frankfurt, EU) and our database by Supabase (London). All connections are encrypted: between your browser and our server (HTTPS), and between our server and the database (SSL).

Deleting your data

Sign in and press Delete my data. This permanently deletes your account, answers and progress straight away.
