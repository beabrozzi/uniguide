Privacy notice

Owl, last updated 7 November 2026

Owl is a student-built prototype. It is not an official university or NHS service and does not give medical advice. If you are in danger or need urgent help, call 999, or call Samaritans free on 116 123.

This notice explains what personal data Owl collects, why, and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

Owl is run by its two founders, students at University College London. We decide how your data is used, which makes us the data controller.

2. We never store health data

Some questions ask about your mental health, physical health, GP registration, health or accessibility needs, allergies, diet and the student communities you're interested in. Your answers to these questions never leave your browser. They are used on your device to build your plan, are never sent to or stored on our server, and are gone when you close or reload the page.

This means we do not store any special-category data, such as health, religion or sexual orientation. Checklist items that would reveal this kind of information (for example, registering with disability support) are not saved either.

3. Using Owl without an account

You can use Owl without an account. Your answers stay in your browser, and none of them are sent to our server.

We use your data only for these purposes. We don't sell or share it, we don't use it for advertising or marketing, and we don't use it to profile you. Your plan is built by simple, fixed rules from your answers. It only suggests services and has no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

5. Who can see it

Only you. Every request to our server checks that you're signed in, and you can only read or change your own data. The founders do not look at individual users' data.

6. Who processes it for us

We use these service providers, who process data only on our instructions:

  • Render: hosts our website and server in Frankfurt, Germany (EU)
  • Supabase: hosts our database in Frankfurt, Germany (EU)
  • Google Fonts: provides the website's font. Your browser fetches it from Google, which receives your IP address. Google may process this outside the UK.

Data stored in the EU is protected by UK "adequacy regulations", which recognise EU data protection law as equivalent to the UK's.

7. How we keep it safe

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, so they can't be read.
  • Every connection is encrypted: your browser to our server (HTTPS), and our server to the database (SSL).
  • Sign-in uses a secure cookie that the page's scripts can't read.
  • Repeated sign-in attempts are rate-limited.

8. Cookies

We use one cookie, ug_session, which keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is strictly necessary for your account to work, so we don't ask for cookie consent. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

9. How long we keep it

  • Saved plan: until you delete your plan or your account, which you can do at any time.
  • Account: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in rate-limit records: about 15 minutes.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period only, for security and fixing errors.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data: sign in and open My account to see your saved plan
  • correct it: use Update my answers, or change your email in My account
  • erase it: in My account, press Delete my plan to permanently delete your saved answers and progress but keep your account, or Delete my account to permanently delete everything. Both happen straight away
  • withdraw consent at any time, by deleting your plan. This doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew
  • restrict or object to how we use your data, and receive your data in a portable format (data portability)

11. Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If you have saved a plan, we will ask for your consent again before any new use.
