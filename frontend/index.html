The problem

The help exists. Students just can't find it in time.

Moving from school to university is when students first manage their own health, food and money. Universities, the NHS and charities all offer support, but it's scattered, and students often find out they qualified only after things went wrong.

41%of UK students felt they weren't made aware of the funding available to themSave the Student, National Student Money Survey 2024 (1,010 UK students)
1 in 4students report no knowledge of mental health services, up from 7% in 2021Cibyl Student Mental Health Study 2024, with Student Minds (12,644 respondents)
73%of students hadn't applied for any financial help from their university, though half reported financial difficultiesOffice for National Statistics, students in England, Nov 2022
How it works

A few questions in, one clear plan out.

1

Tell us about you

7 short screens: health, allergies, budget, housing, studies, schedule, social life. There's nothing to download, and nothing you enter leaves your browser.

2

We match and rank

A rules engine checks your answers against 78 real services and works out what applies to you and what has to come first.

3

You act

Your plan is split into Today, This week and Keep handy. Every item says why it's there, with a direct link to the official page.

      How students get UniGuide

      We reach students where they arrive: their university and their front door.

      Free for students. Licensed by the universities and accommodation providers who support them, and handed out when students are paying the most attention, before term starts.

      🏛️ Universities

      Built into pre-arrival emails, enrolment and welcome week, so students get it from a source they already trust. Each university's own funds, teams and GP partners come first.

      🏢 Accommodation providers

      University halls and private student accommodation: a QR code in the welcome pack and on every room door, introduced on move-in day. One contract with a large provider reaches students from many universities at once.

      🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Resident advisers & Students' Unions

      RAs and resident teams are often the first people a struggling student talks to. UniGuide gives them one link to share, and SUs can promote it at freshers' fairs and through their advice services.

      Why universities pay

      Students dropping out in first year is costly, and continuation rates count in regulation and rankings.

      Why accommodation pays

      Resident wellbeing affects rebookings and reviews, and fewer problems reach staff as crises.

      Anonymous insight

      Aggregate data on unmet need, such as how many new residents have no GP, helps plan support before the rush.
