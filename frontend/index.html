The problem

Students don't lack support. They lack a way to find it.

Starting university means navigating healthcare, finances, wellbeing and independent living, often for the first time. Support is available, but scattered across university websites, public services and charities. Students are left to figure out what exists, what they're eligible for, and where to start.

Mental health
26%of surveyed students and recent graduates didn't know where to seek mental health support.Cibyl Student Mental Health Research, 2025
Healthcare
35%of surveyed students weren't registered with a GP near their place of study.Wonkhe / Cibyl, 2025
Financial support
42%of surveyed students felt unaware of bursaries, grants and scholarships available to them.Save the Student, 2026

      Why partners would pay

      One platform. Value for every partner.

      Universities

      Make existing support easier to discover, reduce repetitive enquiries and help students connect with services before problems escalate.

      Accommodation providers

      Give residents a clearer route to support, reduce routine questions to residence teams and improve the student living experience.

      Business model

      Free for students. Valuable for institutions.

      Universities and accommodation providers already invest in student support. UniOwl aims to make those services easier to discover and access, while reducing repetitive enquiries for support teams.
